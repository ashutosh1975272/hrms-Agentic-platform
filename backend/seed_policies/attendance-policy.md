# Attendance Policy

Version 2.3 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources | Applies to: all employees, including probationers and interns
Related: `leave-policy.md`, `wfh-policy.md`, `code-of-conduct.md`

## 1. Work timings

- Standard day is **09:30 to 18:30 IST**, an 8-hour shift with a 1-hour break.
- The **flexible timing band** permits check-in between **09:00 and 10:00 IST**.
- **Core hours are 11:00 to 15:00 IST**; every employee must be available and reachable then,
  regardless of check-in time. The break window is 13:00 to 15:00 IST.
- On a **5-day week** pattern, Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs. Shift-based employees
  follow the roster published by their Department Head.

## 2. Check-in and check-out

- Attendance is recorded **only** in the HRMS Attendance module. No spreadsheet, email or
  chat message counts as an attendance record.
- The system permits **one check-in and one check-out per day**; the first check-in sets the
  status, and any later check-in is recorded as a re-entry event.
- The **check-in cut-off is 10:00 IST**. A check-in after 10:00 is a **late mark** unless
  approved leave or WFH is recorded for the day.
- Check-out is enabled only after a minimum of **9 hours** of work including breaks.
  Forgetting to check out does not remove the requirement to work the full day.
- On a **WFH day** an employee records one check-in and one check-out from home; no location
  capture is required.

## 3. Late coming, half days and deductions

- **Grace period**: check-in up to 15 minutes late is excused and not recorded.
- **Late mark (LM)**: check-in between 09:46 and 10:45 records half a late mark.
- **Half-day deduction (HD)**: check-in between 10:46 and 12:00, or more than 3 late marks
  in a month, deducts **half a day** from the leave balance.
- **Full-day deduction**: check-in after 12:00, or an absence without approved leave,
  deducts **one full day** from Casual Leave, and from Earned Leave if CL is insufficient.
- Half-day and full-day deductions appear in the monthly attendance statement published on
  the **5th of the following month** in the HRMS.

## 4. Absences

- An unplanned absence of 1 day or more must be reported to the manager and to HR **before
  10:00 IST on the day of absence**, and must be regularised in the HRMS the same day. An
  absence not regularised within **3 days** is treated as Loss of Pay (LOP) for that day.
- **3 consecutive working days** without communication triggers a call from HR; **5
  consecutive working days** without approval is treated as voluntary abandonment of
  service, after a written notice and a 15-day response window.

## 5. Regularisation, holidays and comp-off

- Regularisation for missed check-ins, wrong marks or WFH days must be raised in the HRMS
  **within 3 days**, with a reason and evidence such as a ticket number or a client email.
  Authority: Reporting Manager for 1 day; Reporting Manager plus HR for 2 or more days in a
  month. Requests older than 15 days, or repeated for the same date, are rejected.
- Attendance on a company holiday requires **manager pre-approval recorded in the HRMS at
  least 1 day in advance**. Working a minimum of **6 hours** on a holiday earns **1
  compensatory off**, to be availed within 90 days.
- Compensatory off is not granted for working a weekly off, a leave day, or a company
  holiday, except for on-call staff following the published duty roster. Working a holiday
  without prior approval is treated as a regular office day with no compensatory off.
- A full on-call shift earns 1 compensatory off, and 3 consecutive on-call days earn 1
  additional off.

## 6. Travel, exemption and violations

- Business travel is marked as **Travel** in the HRMS before departure; travel days need no
  check-in or check-out.
- A medical condition certified by a doctor may support an exemption from fixed shift
  timings; exemptions are recorded in the HRMS and reviewed every 6 months.
- Repeated late marks, unmarked absences, or regularisation requests older than 3 days are
  reported to the Department Head and handled under `code-of-conduct.md`. Falsification of
  attendance records is treated as serious misconduct.
