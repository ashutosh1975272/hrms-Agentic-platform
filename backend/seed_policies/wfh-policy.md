# Work From Home (WFH) Policy

Version 2.1 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources | Applies to: all employees on the payroll
Related: `attendance-policy.md`, `leave-policy.md`, `code-of-conduct.md`

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who may work from home, how a request is approved, and the conditions
that apply on remote days. WFH is a flexibility benefit, not a permanent change of work
location, and it never replaces an employee's assigned office days.

## 2. Eligibility (who is eligible)

An employee is eligible to work from home only when **all** of the following are true:

1. Probation of at least **90 days** has been completed.
2. The employee has **6 months of continuous service** from the date of joining.
3. The overall performance rating in the **two most recent appraisal cycles** is "Meets
   Expectations" or higher.
4. There is **no open disciplinary action** and no pending attendance or leave
   regularisation beyond 15 days.
5. The employee's role is **not** listed as ineligible in section 3.

## 3. Ineligible roles

These roles are on-site by default and need Department Head and HR approval for any WFH
day: HR and Payroll staff; IT Helpdesk and Infrastructure support; Finance and Accounts
staff handling cash or vendor payments; Admin, Facilities, Security and Reception staff;
and customer support roles during published business hours.

## 4. Limits

- Maximum **2 WFH days per calendar week**.
- Maximum **3 consecutive WFH days**; 4 or more require Department Head approval.
- WFH is not allowed on company holidays or on the last working day before holiday week.
- WFH days **do not carry over**; unused days expire at the end of the calendar quarter.
- At most **2 WFH requests may be pending approval** at any time.

## 5. Approval flow

1. The employee raises a **WFH request in the HRMS** (Attendance module) with the date and
   an optional reason, at least **24 hours** in advance.
2. The request routes automatically to the **Reporting Manager** for approval.
3. Requests for more than **3 consecutive days** route next to the **Department Head**.
4. Any request breaching the 2-day weekly limit routes to **HR**, which may approve it as a
   recorded exception.
5. The request **auto-escalates to the manager** if not actioned within 12 hours.
6. The employee receives an email and in-app notification of the final status. A rejection
   must state a reason.
7. **Retrospective requests are not permitted**, except for medical emergencies approved by
   HR within 7 days of the date worked.

## 6. Working conditions and attendance

- Core collaboration hours are **11:00 to 15:00 IST**; the employee must be reachable and
  set to **Available** in the chat application for the full core window.
- The employee must have a company laptop, VPN access and a stable internet connection.
- Only a **WFH-assigned desk** may be used for confidential calls. Cubicles and shared spaces
  are not suitable for handling employee or customer data. Team meetings must be by video.
- WFH days follow the same timings, late marking and absence rules as office days, recorded
  by a single **check-in at the start of the day and check-out at the end of the day**. No
  location capture is required; thresholds are defined in `attendance-policy.md`.

## 7. Data protection and security

- Access to production systems from home requires VPN and is monitored.
- Customer and employee data must not be printed, copied to personal devices or stored
  locally, and devices must not be shared with family members.
- Loss or theft of a company device on a WFH day must be reported to IT and HR within
  **4 hours**; repeated incidents may revoke WFH eligibility.

## 8. Reverting to office and enforcement

WFH eligibility is reviewed every quarter by HR. WFH may be suspended with 1 week's notice
when attendance or performance declines, a client engagement requires on-site presence, a
delivery commitment is at risk, or a security incident occurred on a remote day; the
suspension is recorded in the HRMS and shared with the employee and their manager. Written
exceptions from HR and the Department Head are granted for a maximum of 1 quarter.
Working from home without an approved request is an attendance violation under
`code-of-conduct.md`.
