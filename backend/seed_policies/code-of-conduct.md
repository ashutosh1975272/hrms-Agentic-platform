# Code of Conduct

Version 2.0 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources and Compliance
Applies to: all employees, contractors, interns and vendors on company premises or systems
Related: `benefits.md`, `attendance-policy.md`, `travel-policy.md`

## 1. Commitment and expected behaviour

The company is committed to a workplace that is safe, respectful, inclusive and free from
harassment and discrimination. Every employee is expected to uphold these standards whether
working on site, from home, at a client location, or at a company event. Specifically:

- Treat colleagues, clients, candidates and visitors with dignity and respect, communicate
  professionally in every channel, and give feedback on work rather than on identity.
- Honour commitments, disclose conflicts of interest, and keep confidential information
  secure.
- Comply with all company policies, including `attendance-policy.md`, `leave-policy.md` and
  `wfh-policy.md`, work from approved locations, and observe the defined working hours.
- Report safety hazards, misconduct and conflicts of interest promptly.

## 2. Discrimination and harassment

Discrimination on the basis of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, religion,
caste, race, disability, marital status, pregnancy, or any other legally protected
characteristic is prohibited. This includes verbal abuse, unwelcome physical contact,
visual material, jokes and messages, and any conduct that creates an intimidating or hostile
work environment. **Rape, sexual harassment and physical assault are reportable offences.**
The company follows a zero tolerance policy and supports the complainant throughout.

## 3. Reporting channels

Any employee may report a concern in confidence through any of these channels:

1. Their **Reporting Manager**.
2. The **HR team**.
3. The **Ethics Helpline**, available 24x7, with anonymous reporting enabled.
4. The **POSH committee** for workplace harassment complaints.

Complaints are investigated by a committee of at least 2 members and are normally concluded
within **21 days**. The identity of the complainant is protected. **Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.**

## 4. Confidentiality, data and conflicts

- Employee, client and company data may be used only for authorised business purposes.
  Confidential material includes source code, financial data, HR records, salary data and
  customer information. It must not be copied to personal devices, personal email or public
  cloud storage, and nothing may be posted publicly about company business. Breach of
  confidentiality is a serious misconduct regardless of intent.
- An employee must disclose in writing to HR any situation where a personal interest conflicts
  with the interests of the company or a client. This applies to employment with a competitor,
  shareholding in a supplier, a board membership, or a close family member in a reporting
  relationship. Failing to disclose a conflict is a disciplinary offence.

## 5. Gifts, resources and social media

- Gifts and hospitality above **INR 2,000** must be declined or returned and reported to
  Compliance. Cash, gift cards and personal discounts are never acceptable, regardless of
  value. Donations are made only through the company's giving programme.
- Company laptops, email, chat and networks are for business use; occasional personal use is
  permitted if it does not interfere with work or risk the company. Software must be licensed
  and installed only through IT. Unauthorised tools, personal VPN clients and unapproved AI
  services must not be used with company data, and `wfh-policy.md` applies to remote work.
- Only authorised spokespeople may make public statements on behalf of the company. Posting
  confidential material, customer information or internal correspondence on social media is a
  confidentiality breach, and personal posts must not defame the company or its clients.

## 6. Disciplinary process

Misconduct is handled in proportion to its seriousness: a **verbal warning** for a first
minor violation recorded by the manager; a **written warning** for a repeated or moderately
serious violation issued by HR; a **final warning** with up to 3 months of probation and a
documented improvement plan; **suspension pending investigation** for up to 10 working days;
and **termination of service** for serious misconduct, including harassment, theft, fraud,
falsification of records and breach of confidentiality.

The employee receives written notice of the allegation and an opportunity to respond before
any sanction, except where there is immediate danger. A recovery amount may be applied where
an employee resigns while under disciplinary process or in breach of notice. Every case is
recorded in the HRMS audit log.
