# Employee Benefits Guide

Version 1.6 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources and Total Rewards
Applies to: all employees on permanent and fixed-term contracts
Related: `leave-policy.md`, `code-of-conduct.md`, `travel-policy.md`

## 1. Statutory benefits

This guide summarises the benefits available to employees. Statutory benefits are provided
as required by law; additional benefits are listed in sections 2 to 7. The signed employment
contract takes precedence over this summary.

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Provident Fund (PF) | 12% of basic salary, employee and employer each, per EPF rules |
| ESI | Applies to employees earning below the statutory wage ceiling |
| Gratuity | After 5 or more years of continuous service, 15 days of last drawn basic per year |
| Statutory bonus | 8.33% of eligible annual pay, payable within 8 months of financial year end |
| Labour welfare fund | As per the state contribution for the work location |

## 2. Insurance and medical

- **Group term life insurance**: **INR 10,00,000** per employee, rising to 4 times annual
  basic salary after 1 year of service.
- **Group medical insurance**: family floater cover of **INR 5,00,000** for the employee,
  spouse, up to 2 children and parents, with a 24-month pre-existing disease waiting period.
  **Dental and vision care** is an optional add-on of INR 5,000 cover per family.
- Claims must be intimated to the TPA within **30 days** of hospitalisation, and within
  **60 days** for cashless claims. An employee may opt out only during the December
  enrolment window and must submit proof of alternative cover.

## 3. Retirement, health and wellbeing

- Voluntary Provident Fund contribution up to the statutory ceiling, plus additional
  voluntary contribution where permitted. **Employee stock options** are offered at the E1
  band and above, with a 1-year cliff and 4-year vesting; grant letters and vesting dates are
  published in the HRMS. Employer contribution to the long-term incentive plan is made for
  senior employees.
- **Mental health support**: 12 free counselling sessions per year per employee through the
  Employee Assistance Programme, plus 6 sessions for family members.
- **Fitness reimbursement**: up to **INR 3,000 per month** against a gym or fitness class
  receipt, claimed through the HRMS expenses module.
- **Annual health check-up** for employees above 35 years of age or with 3 or more years of
  service. **Bereavement leave**: 2 days paid for immediate family and 1 day for extended
  family, in addition to the entitlements in `leave-policy.md`.

## 4. Family, parental and learning benefits

- Maternity leave of **26 weeks** and paternity leave of **2 weeks** as per `leave-policy.md`,
  with crèche assistance of **INR 6,000 per month** while both parents are employed.
- A **one-time joining bonus of INR 25,000** for women employees returning from a career
  break of 2 or more years, with WFH limits as per `wfh-policy.md`. **Child care leave** of
  12 days per year is available to parents of children below 12 years of age.
- An annual **learning budget of INR 20,000** for certification courses, workshops and books,
  released on 1 January and requiring manager approval before purchase, plus **5 days of
  paid training leave** carryable forward for one year. Sponsorship of professional
  memberships and conferences is granted at the E2 band and above on Department Head approval.

## 5. Workplace perks

- A **meal allowance of INR 30 per working day**, credited monthly, and a **transport
  allowance of INR 1,600 per month** or a one-time reimbursement of INR 8,000 per year for
  employees who travel to the office.
- Flexible seating and a two-monitor workstation for all employees; WFH employees receive an
  **IT equipment allowance of INR 8,000 per year**.
- Access to the internal employee store, discount cards, and the annual health and engagement
  events.

## 6. Eligibility, enrolment and claims

- Benefits are effective from the **first day of the month after joining**, and new employees
  must complete the **benefits enrolment form in the HRMS within 15 days** of joining. Late
  enrolment is accepted only for statutory benefits.
- Insurance and retirement details are treated as confidential personal data and are never
  shared outside HR without consent. Every claim requires a valid receipt or certificate, and
  claims older than 90 days are not processed.
- Eligibility and claim queries are raised with HR through the HRMS helpdesk, which responds
  within 3 working days. This guide is informational and creates no entitlement beyond the
  signed employment contract.
