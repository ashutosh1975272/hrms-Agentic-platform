# Leave Policy

Version 3.0 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources | Applies to: all employees, including probationers
Related: `attendance-policy.md`, `wfh-policy.md`, `benefits.md`

## 1. Scope and entitlements

This policy covers annual, sick, casual, work-from-home, parental and compensatory leave.
Entitlements are **pro-rated** for employees who join mid-year, rounded to the nearest half
day. Public holidays follow the holiday calendar published every December in the HRMS.

| Leave type | Entitlement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 12 days | Granted at the start of the year, 1 per month |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days | Granted at the start of the year, 1 per month |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 15 days | Accrues at 1.25 days per completed month |
| Work From Home (WFH) | 2 days per week | Separate limit, see `wfh-policy.md` |
| Maternity Leave (ML) | 26 weeks | For eligible women employees |
| Paternity Leave (PL) | 2 weeks | Within 3 months of childbirth or adoption |
| Compensatory Off | Earned basis | 1 day per weekend or public holiday worked |
| Marriage Leave | 7 days | One-time, within 3 months of marriage |
| Child Care Leave | 12 days | Parents of children under 12, separate from CL/SL/EL |

## 2. Accrual and carry forward

- Earned Leave is credited to the balance on the **1st day of each month** for the month
  just completed. Carry forward at year end is limited to **15 days** of EL, CL and SL
  combined; the excess lapses on 31 December.
- Casual Leave must be applied for at least **2 hours in advance** except in emergencies.
- Sick leave of 1 day or more must be informed to the manager **before 10:00 IST** on the
  day of absence. A medical certificate is required for **2 or more consecutive days** and
  must be uploaded in the HRMS within **7 days**.
- **Family sick leave** is limited to **6 days per year** for the illness of parents,
  spouses, children or dependent parents.

## 3. Application and approval flow

1. All leave is applied for through the **HRMS Leave module**. Verbal or email requests are
   not accepted in place of an HRMS request.
2. Requests of **3 days or fewer** need the **Reporting Manager's** approval only.
3. Requests of **4 days or more** need the Reporting Manager plus a **countersign from HR**.
4. Requests of **10 consecutive days or more** additionally need **Department Head** approval.
5. Managers must action a request within **24 hours**; requests pending beyond 48 hours
   escalate automatically to the manager's manager.
6. A request is rejected only with a stated reason, after which it may be modified and
   resubmitted.

## 4. Public holidays and cancellation

- A public holiday falling inside a leave period is **not deducted** from the leave balance.
  A weekly off or public holiday inside leave is deducted unless it was applied as **leave
  without pay (LWP)**. Compensatory off earned for working a holiday expires if not availed
  within **90 days**.
- A **pending** request may be cancelled at any time before approval. An **approved** request
  may be cancelled up to **48 hours** before the start date, with the balance adjusted.
- Cancellation within 48 hours of the start date needs manager approval, except for medical
  emergencies, which need manager approval and a medical certificate. Leave already availed
  cannot be cancelled and must be regularised as LWP if it cannot be adjusted against the
  next accrual.

## 5. Long leave, parental leave and exceptions

- **Leave without pay** is granted for a maximum of 90 days in a year on Department Head and
  HR approval. Salary is not paid and benefits are suspended pro-rata. An unpaid
  **sabbatical of up to 8 weeks** is available once every 3 years to employees with at least
  5 years of service, against a commitment to return for 12 months.
- Maternity leave of 26 weeks may be taken in up to 2 blocks, with at least 2 weeks
  continuous before the expected delivery date and 6 weeks after, followed by an additional
  **12 weeks of child care leave**. Paternity leave of 2 weeks must be taken within 3 months
  of childbirth, and 15 days of it within the first month. Both require **30 days' written
  notice to HR**, and the medical or birth certificate is recorded in the employee profile.
- Probationers receive pro-rata CL, SL and EL, but are not eligible for sabbatical or
  marriage leave until confirmation. An employee serving notice may take approved leave, but
  may not resign and join elsewhere during the notice period; the full recovery amount applies.
- Balance discrepancies must be raised with HR within **30 days** of the accrual date,
  quoting the leave request ID. Any exception needs written HR Head approval.
