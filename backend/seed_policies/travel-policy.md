# Business Travel Policy

Version 1.4 | Effective 01 April 2025 | Owner: Finance and Human Resources
Applies to: all employees travelling on company business
Related: `benefits.md`, `attendance-policy.md`, `code-of-conduct.md`

## 1. Purpose and travel authorisation

This policy covers authorisation, booking, expenses, safety and reimbursement for domestic
and international business travel. Personal travel extended with a business trip must be
declared in writing to HR and the Tax team before departure, as different rules apply.

1. Every trip must be **pre-approved in the HRMS Travel module** by the Reporting Manager,
   with the purpose, dates and cost centre recorded. Trips over **INR 50,000** also require
   **Department Head approval**, and international trips additionally require **Finance
   approval**. Requests are raised **5 working days** before departure, or 10 for visa travel.
2. An approved travel request must exist in the HRMS before any expense claim is accepted;
   trips booked without it are reimbursed only with written Department Head and Finance Head
   approval. Travel dates are marked as **Travel** in the HRMS Attendance module, with no
   check-in and check-out required.

## 2. Mode of travel and class of service

- Under **6 hours**: **Economy class** by air, or AC 3 Tier / Sleeper rail for journeys under
  12 hours. Over **6 hours**: **Premium Economy** is permitted for all employees; **Business
  class** is permitted only for the **E2 band and above** travelling over 8 hours.
- Rail journeys exceeding 24 hours are not approved; fly instead. Company cars are for local
  travel only, and personal vehicles are reimbursed at **INR 12 per kilometre** where none is
  available. **Travel insurance** is mandatory internationally, arranged by the Travel Desk.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotels are booked through the Travel Desk portal and reimbursed within the nightly cap:

| City tier | Example cities | Nightly cap |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad | INR 6,000 |
| Tier 2 | Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow, Ahmedabad | INR 4,000 |
| Tier 3 | All other locations | INR 3,000 |
| International | Per destination | USD 200 |

Bookings above the cap require Department Head approval. A stay beyond 7 nights is reviewed
weekly and the travel plan must be updated or the trip concluded.

## 4. Per diem and local conveyance

- **Domestic per diem**: **INR 800 per day** for Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities, **INR 600 per
  day** for Tier 3, with company-arranged meals deducted. **International per diem**: **USD
  60 per day**, covering meals and local transport but not payable on travel-only days within
  the city of the registered office.
- Alcohol, personal entertainment and minibar charges are not reimbursable. Airport and
  station transfers are booked by the Travel Desk or reimbursed on actuals with a receipt.
  Client-visit cabs are reimbursed on actuals, and self-driven client visits at INR 12 per km.

## 5. Expenses, passport and visa

- Claims must be filed in the HRMS expenses module within **30 days** of returning; claims
  older than 60 days require Finance Head approval. Every claim line requires a **GST
  invoice** and the **Purpose code** declared at booking, backed by original receipts.
- The company pays the **visa, travel insurance, vaccination and mandatory medical tests**
  required for international travel. Advance payments are limited to **INR 20,000** per trip
  and are settled against the final claim. Personal expenses, alcohol, minibar, in-flight
  upgrades beyond policy, and expenses of accompanying family members are not reimbursable.
- The Travel Desk files the visa application; the passport must be submitted at least **21
  days** before travel and be valid for **6 months** beyond the travel date. A refusal caused
  by a false declaration leads to recovery of costs and disciplinary action, and loss of a
  passport while travelling must be reported to the police and the company within **24 hours**.

## 6. Safety, duties of care and cancellations

- The employee registers the itinerary with the Travel Desk, keeps a charged phone, and
  carries a copy of the visa and insurance documents. A destination with a government travel
  advisory requires Department Head approval and a security briefing. Employees travel with
  client and vendor professionalism and comply with `code-of-conduct.md` at all times. Any
  injury or illness must be reported to HR immediately, and medical costs are covered by
  group insurance.
- Flights and hotels booked through the Travel Desk must be cancelled through the same portal
  so a credit note is raised. Cancellations within 24 hours of departure attract a
  non-refundable cost deducted from the employee's claim, unless manager-approved or caused
  by company action. Repeated no-shows may result in cash-and-carry booking for later trips.
